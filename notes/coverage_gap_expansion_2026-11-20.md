## Most useful reading paths

### Propellant material research
Start with motor casting rheology (RSP-111), quality controls (RSP-112), polymer mechanical behavior (RSP-114), and dynamic characterization (RSP-115). RSP-113 is an **abstract only**, but provides a lead on long-duration material aging. RSP-115 and RSP-116 offer downloadable reports on dynamic response and motor structural failure limits.

### Condensed-phase combustion products
RSP-118 and RSP-119 examine effluent products and aluminized propellant combustion under acceleration, while RSP-121 provides plume particle measurements from static firings. This section supplements existing DLR alumina-particle studies.

### Structural integrity and experimental validation
RSP-123 is a foundational NASA grain-integrity publication; RSP-124 assesses inspection capabilities and limitations; RSP-127–128 concern modal dynamics and experimentally verified structural failure criteria. The model-validation records include laboratory heat transfer, acoustic stability, IR plume diagnostics, and system identification; **RSP-129 and RSP-130 are abstracts**, not complete papers.

### Flight systems
RSP-152–156 cover onboard IMU/GNSS fusion, multi-frequency GNSS receivers, spin-stabilized rocket navigation, and fault-tolerant navigation architecture. The recovery group (RSP-134–139) spans system identification, flight reconstruction, deployment systems, and sounding-rocket mission recovery.

### Beyond solid motors
RSP-140–145 cover hybrid regression, flowfield CFD, transient combustion, and experimental test data. RSP-146–151 introduce pressure-fed liquid propulsion, injector guidelines, feed-system dynamics, and test results.

## Verification and collection constraints

1. Bibliographic verification: the NASA records were fetched through the official NTRS metadata API; DLR records were read from original DLR repository pages.
2. Full-text verification: **not performed** during this expansion. `pdf_status = "not downloaded"` for PDF-linked references and `pdf_status = "not available"` where none was provided.
3. Author metadata: sourced from NTRS author affiliations and DLR item descriptions.
4. Bibliography PDFs may be subject to rights and redistribution conditions; the public repository stores links and metadata, not third-party PDF files.
5. The original 110 entries contain additional metadata gaps; this expansion also backfilled 20 previously missing publication years. A full citation and duplicate-identity audit remains desirable.
