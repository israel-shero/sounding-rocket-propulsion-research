## Reading priorities

1. **Motor slag and two-phase effects:** RSP-157–158 provide numerical and experimental context for molten/condensed particulate dynamics in solid-motor flow.
2. **Aging and quality assurance:** RSP-160, RSP-171 and RSP-172 address material storage and non-destructive material checks.
3. **Flight reconstruction:** RSP-161–165 cover sounding-rocket demonstrations, aerodynamic decelerators, and post-flight trajectory analysis.
4. **Experimental data analysis:** RSP-176–178 include open research on optical regression measurement and test anomaly detection.
5. **Newer engine research:** DLR studies from 2024–2026 cover thermoacoustics, CFD, alternative fuels, and emission accounting.
